# Chebyshev Expansion Examples

These examples illuminate the mathematical foundations of chebfunjax —
the Chebyshev polynomials themselves, the FFT connection, and the
theory of polynomial approximation in the Chebyshev basis.

| Example | Description |
|---------|-------------|

| [Explaining chebfun construction](ChebExplain.md) | translation: annotated coefficient plots of constructor decisions; eps=1e-8 construction digit-for-digit. |
| [Chebyshev polynomials as plotted by Fornberg and Higham](ChebPolysHigham.md) | translation: the 3D waterfall plots of T_k and P_k. |
| [Convergence for fractional smoothness](Convergence.md) | translation: n^-pi and n^-5.5 interpolation convergence rates. |
| [Exact Chebyshev coefficients](ExactChebCoeffs.md) | translation: closed-form geometric coefficients of 1/(5+x) digit-for-digit. |
| [Turbocharged Chebyshev coefficients](Turbo.md) | translation: turbo doubles the coefficients; 10th-derivative and complex-evaluation payoffs match published. |
| [The doublelength flag](DoublelengthFlag.md) | translation: coefficient plateaus below the chop point for cheb and trig constructions. |
| [Fast Chebyshev-Legendre transform](FastChebyshevLegendreTransform.md) | translation: Chebyshev vs Legendre coefficient families for geometric and algebraic decay. |
| [Fast discrete Legendre transform](FastDLT.md) | translation: DLT at N=1e4 in under a second; roundtrip 2.2e-15. |
